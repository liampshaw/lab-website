title: "Computational Microbiology Lab"
---



We are a research group at the [University of Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk),
led by Liam Shaw. We study the evolutionary biology of bacteria using
computational approaches. 

Our work focuses on understanding how bacteria evolve. We work on topics like antibiotic resistance, plasmid biology, bacterial immunity and metagenomics.  

For more of an introduction to our work, see our [research](/research).
