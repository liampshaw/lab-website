### New paper out on plasmid biology

**17 August 2026**  
A paper of Liam's has been published in *Nucleic Acids Research*. In it, we show that the leading region of plasmids - the first part that enters a new cell - has a special form of adaptation to defence systems that has previously gone unnoticed. The earliest genes in the plasmid seem to have been selected to use the genetic code in a way that maximises the speed at which they make proteins to combat host defences. You can read the paper [here](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag810).

### Resistance potentiators
**6 July 2026**    
Together with Craig MacLean and Adam Mulkern (Oxford), an essay in *PLOS Biology* arguing that antibiotic resistance is often catalyzed by what we call 'resistance potentiators'. Introducing a new concept is always tricky, but we think that this one does useful work in uniting together an assortment of different mechanisms by which bacteria can accelerate evolution in a trait-specific manner. Read the paper [here](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3003852).
