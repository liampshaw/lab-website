### New paper out

**17 August 2026** A paper of Liam's has been published in *Nucleic Acids Research*. In it, we show that the leading region of plasmids - the first part that enters a new cell - has a special form of adaptation that has previously gone unnoticed. You can read the paper [here](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag810)
